# Privacy Policy — ABC Phonics Kids

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application **ABC Phonics Kids** ("the App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. About the App

ABC Phonics Kids is an educational app that teaches English phonics to children aged 3–8 through interactive listening activities, letter sounds, blending, and short stories. The App is designed primarily for children and complies with:

- **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** — United States
- **General Data Protection Regulation — Article 8 (GDPR-K)** — European Union / United Kingdom
- **Google Play Families Policy**
- **Apple App Store — Kids Category Guidelines**

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We are committed to collecting the **minimum amount of information necessary** to provide our service. We **do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from children**.

### 2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

- Full name, real name, or nickname tied to identity
- Home address, postal address, or geolocation
- Phone number or email address from the child
- Photographs, videos, or contact lists
- Audio recordings of the child's voice (the App is currently listening-only)
- Social media profiles or login information
- Government identifiers
- Precise GPS location

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected (Non-Personal)

The App may automatically collect the following **non-personal, anonymized technical information** to operate, secure, and improve the App:

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device information | Device model, OS version, screen size, language, country | Optimize UI, debug crashes |
| App information | App version, build number | Compatibility, bug fixes |
| Usage information | Lesson progress, level completed, in-app preferences | Save your progress locally |
| Diagnostic data | Crash logs, error reports (no personal data) | Fix bugs |
| Advertising identifier (Android AAID / iOS IDFA) | Used **only** to serve **non-personalized ads** if ads are shown to mixed-audience users (see §4) | — |

All progress and preferences are stored **locally on the device** (using on-device storage) and are not transmitted to our servers.

### 2.3 Information from Parents (Optional)

If a parent contacts us for support, deletion requests, or account questions, we may receive:

- Parent's email address
- Content of the email (description of the issue)

This information is used **only to respond to the request** and is deleted within 90 days after the issue is resolved.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information collected to:

1. Operate, maintain, and improve the App
2. Save in-app progress locally on the device
3. Display age-appropriate, non-personalized advertising (where applicable)
4. Process in-app purchases through the platform store (Google Play / App Store)
5. Diagnose technical problems and prevent abuse
6. Comply with legal obligations

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 4. Advertising

If the App displays advertisements, we use ad networks that are certified for **child-directed** or **mixed-audience** apps under the Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

- **All ads shown to children are non-personalized (contextual only).**
- Ads do **not** use behavioral profiling, retargeting, or interest-based tracking for users under 13.
- Ads do **not** request personal information.
- Ads do **not** link out to social media, web browsers, or app stores without a parental gate where required.
- Ads are filtered for age-appropriate content (no alcohol, gambling, violence, dating, mature themes).

You can reset the advertising identifier at any time through your device settings:
- **Android:** Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

---

## 5. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases (e.g., premium content, ad removal). All purchases:

- Are gated behind a **parental gate** (e.g., math challenge or hold-to-confirm) before checkout
- Are processed entirely by **Google Play Billing** or **Apple App Store**
- Use **RevenueCat** as the receipt-validation layer
- Are subject to the respective store's refund policy

We do **not** store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment details. We receive only an anonymous transaction identifier from the store.

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The App is **directed to children under 13** (and under 16 in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom).

We comply fully with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) and **Article 8 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**.

Our commitments:

1. We do **not** knowingly collect personal information from any child.

## 8. Data Storage & Security

- **Local-first:** All in-app progress, preferences, and lesson state are stored **on the device** (Room database, encrypted system storage).
- **No user accounts:** The App does not require sign-up, login, or any account creation.
- **No cloud sync of personal data.**
- **Transport security:** Any network requests (e.g., downloading audio assets from Firebase Storage) use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- **Audio content** is static, pre-recorded curriculum content. No user-generated audio is uploaded.

We retain anonymized diagnostic data for a maximum of **90 days**, after which it is automatically deleted.
